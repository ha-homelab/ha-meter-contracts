# Troubleshooting

For scope_outside, do not assign a whole-strip reading to one outlet. For duplicate_stream, retain one representation in a sum. For stale or missing samples, restore source data without inventing zeros. For HA import, map a real source-time attribute. Never publish a complete household export in an issue.
