Offline validation of physical meter meaning, additive topology and sample quality. No device connection or commands.

- [Quick start](https://github.com/ha-homelab/ha-meter-contracts/wiki/Usage)
- [Contract and report](https://github.com/ha-homelab/ha-meter-contracts/wiki/Contract)
- [Troubleshooting](https://github.com/ha-homelab/ha-meter-contracts/wiki/Troubleshooting)

A whole-strip meter is one aggregate. An MQTT copy is not a second independent meter. Unavailable is not zero. These distinctions are the core of the first release.

Wiki sources are versioned in the main repository. The complete guides are available in 24 documentation languages under `docs/` in the same release; wiki pages link to those canonical guides. The CLI retains its four supported message languages.
